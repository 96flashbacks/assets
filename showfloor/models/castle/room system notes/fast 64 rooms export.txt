Fast64 properly exports the rooms in the sense that the surface triangles are properly linked to only one room ID, the issue is that Fast64 doesn't export the rooms in the proper order, so it needs to be manually fixed. It's as simple as replacing the rooms IDs with the proper ones (just slightly tedious), the other two txts give notes on how to replace the room IDs, I'd recommend first replacing all the numbers with letters and then replace the letters with the proper room IDs. an example folder is included to show how to fix the room IDs

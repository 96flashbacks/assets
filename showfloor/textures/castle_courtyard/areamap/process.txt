1. Make two renders with the blend "courtyard_areamap.blend", one with the water and one without the water.
2. Using Photoshop, downscale both images to 64x64 with bilinear resampling.
3. Get rid of transparent pixels (I've done it with Photoshop going Layer > Layer Mask > From Transparency then Image > Adjustments > Threshold… (Threshold Level at 1).
4. Overlay the render with the water above the one without, and set the opacity of the render with water to 50%.
5. Include the "transparency" layer above both renders.
